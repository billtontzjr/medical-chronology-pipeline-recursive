More review tools

These advanced actions require a source-review note.

Source-backed correction

This replaces the entire page’s extracted text on the next run. Enter all readable text from that page, not just one corrected word. Leave it blank for a name-spacing or date-validation issue. Original files and the prior extraction are preserved.

Manually reviewed chronology entry

Choose “Include a paragraph I verified from the original” above and identify the physical PDF page. This replaces generated content linked to that page only. Other unread pages remain flagged. Enter only what you personally verified; missing provider or facility can use the placeholders below. This is saved as a named human review, not an AI verification.
